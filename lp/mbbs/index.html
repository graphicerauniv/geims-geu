MBBS

Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery

The MBBS (Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery) program at GEIMS, the best MBBS college in Dehradun is meticulously designed to cultivate competent, compassionate, and skilled medical professionals. At Graphic Era Institute of Medical Sciences, excellence is not just a goal; it is our foundation. Since we are one of the top notch MBBS colleges in India, our distinguished faculty comprises leaders in their fields who are dedicated to imparting cutting-edge knowledge and practical skills. From anatomy to advanced clinical practices, our curriculum is designed to equip you with the expertise needed to excel in the dynamic world of medicine

The MBBS program at GEIMS follows a structured curriculum that spans over 5.6 years, including a one-year compulsory rotatory internship. The course is divided into pre-clinical, para-clinical, and clinical phases:

  • Pre-Clinical Phase: This phase covers the basic sciences, including Anatomy, Physiology, and Biochemistry. Students gain a solid understanding of the human body’s structure and function.
  • Para-Clinical Phase: During this phase, students study subjects like Pathology, Microbiology, Pharmacology, and Forensic Medicine. This phase bridges the gap between basic sciences and clinical subjects.
  • Clinical Phase: The clinical phase focuses on practical training in various medical disciplines such as Medicine, Surgery, Pediatrics, Obstetrics & Gynecology, Psychiatry, Dermatology, and more. Students rotate through different departments to gain hands-on experience in patient care.

Four and a half academic years plus one year of Compulsory Rotatory Residential Internship (CRRI), NEET UG Qualified.
